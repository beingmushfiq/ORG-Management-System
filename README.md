## 🏛️ Executive Overview & Purpose

The **Road Safety Movement (নিরাপদ সড়ক আন্দোলন — বাংলাদেশ)** is Bangladesh's apex grassroots citizen alliance and youth mobilization platform. Born from the historic **August 2018 student demonstrations** that united millions across Dhaka and all 64 districts following the tragic loss of two college students on Airport Road, the movement represents a generational demand for accountability, justice, and transit dignity.

### The Problem
Bangladesh loses over **8,000+ citizens each year** to preventable road violence, with tens of thousands suffering life-altering trauma. Decades of uncoordinated enforcement, fake vehicular fitness certifications, predatory speeding on national expressways, unmapped highway blackspots, and the absence of golden-hour trauma triage have made Bangladesh's transit corridors among the most hazardous in South Asia.

### The Solution: Vision Zero Bangladesh 2030
The **ORG Management System** equips the movement with an enterprise-grade digital command center designed to turn civic grief into systemic change:
- **64 District Coordination Cells**: Unified digital chapters spanning all 8 administrative divisions.
- **50,000+ Enlisted Youth Cadets**: Active roadside monitors, pedestrian crossing patrols, and public transit observers.
- **420+ High-Fatality Highway Blackspots**: GIS-mapped risk nodes tracked with live crash telemetry and infrastructure repair status.
- **1,200+ Golden-Hour Certified First-Aid Responders**: Trained youth volunteers stationed along major highway corridors to deliver critical medical intervention within the first 60 minutes of a crash.
- **Institutional Alliances**: Connected with BUET Accident Research Institute (ARI), Highway Police HQ, BRTA, Fire Service & Civil Defence, and Bangladesh Red Crescent Society.

## 🛑 Mission & Terminological Invariant ("Road Crash")

In accordance with global transit science, the World Health Organization (WHO), and Vision Zero principles:
- ❌ **We do not use the term "Accident"**: The word *accident* implies that traffic fatalities are unpredictable, inevitable acts of God or unavoidable bad luck.
- ✅ **We strictly enforce the term "Road Crash" (সড়ক দুর্ঘটনা / সড়ক সংঘর্ষ)**: Over 98% of road deaths are entirely preventable engineering, behavioral, or regulatory failures — caused by speeding, unroadworthy vehicles, driver exhaustion, road curvature flaws, or missing pedestrian infrastructure. Treating crashes as preventable failures is the foundation of Vision Zero.

*Located at `resources/js/Components/3D/BangladeshGlobe.jsx`*

- **Risk Classification Badges**: Automatic visual categorization of districts into *High Risk* (red pulse), *Medium Risk* (amber pulse), and *Low Risk* (green pulse).

*Located at `resources/js/Pages/Portal/CmsStudio.jsx`*

  4. *Governance Hierarchy*: 5-Tier RBAC directorate management and national organogram builder.
  5. *Strategic Alliances & Policies*: Institutional partners marquee, public FAQs, and statutory terms.
- **Section Visibility Switchboard**: 1-click toggles enabling or disabling public homepage sections dynamically.

### 4. Volunteer Cadet & Member Registration Flow
*Located at `resources/js/Pages/Public/Apply.jsx`*
- **Guided Multi-Step Wizard**:
  - *Tier Selection*: Volunteer Cadet (BDT 70) vs. General Member (BDT 100).
  - *District Chapter Assignment*: Direct selection from all 64 districts.
  - *Statutory Policies Checkbox*: Mandatory acceptance of Terms & Conditions, Refund Policy, Privacy Policy, and Delivery Policy.
  - *SSLCommerz Payment Gateway*: Seamless checkout with bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Visa, Mastercard, and Amex.
- **Cryptographic E-Credential**: Instant generation of verified digital membership cards with scannable QR verification.

### 5. Zero Horizontal Scrolling Guarantee

```bash
# 1. Navigate to the Laravel application directory
cd "d:\ORG Management System\agri-laravel"

composer install

# 3. Install frontend JavaScript dependencies
npm install

# 4. Copy environment configuration
cp .env.example .env

# 5. Generate application cryptographic key
php artisan key:generate
```
